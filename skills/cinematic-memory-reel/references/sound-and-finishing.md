# Sound and finishing

## Connect sound to picture

Record each excerpt's source ID, source in/out, timeline range, gain, filtering, fades and listening/check status.

At ordinary speed, timeline time `t` maps to `source_in + (t - timeline_start)`. If picture is retimed or replaced, update or deliberately exclude its source sound. Do not leave an old recording under a new shot by accident.

Use a clean partial excerpt with the corresponding visible source moment. If useful, change the visual source in-point to align them. Fade inside the usable range rather than extending into a noisy passage. Photographs do not automatically acquire another location's ambience.

Keep music at its intended pace unless the brief calls for a change. Volume ducking is not a tempo change. A small local reduction with short ramps can reveal a natural moment without stopping the music. Inspect adjacent ducks so the level does not flutter between nearby clips.

Keep sound, music and requested effects separately editable. Mute unused original video audio, including nested layers. A no-music alternate can retain deliberately selected source sound for later music replacement.

## Understand the evidence

Listening is the primary perceptual check. VAD can mistake water or wind for speech; transcription can hallucinate words over noise or miss faint voices. Zero detections do not certify voice-free audio, and a detection is not automatically proof of speech. Inspect flagged passages and disclose uncertainty when actual listening is unavailable.

Filtering can reduce rumble or hiss; it is not a general voice remover. Exclude unsuitable passages rather than pretending they are clean. Generated ambience or a constant sound bed is a creative choice requiring the appropriate brief.

Compare a native render to its intended stems and automation when deterministic verification would help. Agreement detects routing/timing errors, not contamination already present in the chosen source.

## Work in the real environment

Use available native controls or documented APIs. Confirm the intended project, preserve unsaved work, and target only that session. Never reuse an example's app process ID or absolute path.

A visible filename can still be a cloud placeholder. Verify readability. For inconsistent or truncated reads, check source integrity and stage local copies. Collect final project links inside the delivery folder, rather than leaving them pointed at a disposable cache.

Check the actual encoded frame count, duration, streams and complete decode. A completion banner alone is insufficient. Inspect the encoded opening, ending, transitions, title and demanding crops. Contact sheets do not verify intermediate motion or sound.

## Deliver and clean

Package the approved export, requested caption/cover, editable native project and linked media, a concise usage note and a tested ZIP. Add a no-music alternate when useful. Avoid several ambiguous files all called final.

State whether music is the selected recording, a licensed replacement, an original composition or a temporary guide. Do not redistribute proprietary LUT installers, templates or commercial recordings merely because the editor could access them.

Clean only the authorized scope after verifying retained deliverables. Prefer recoverable Trash moves. Source uploads and other projects are not implicitly disposable. Publishing this reusable skill does not authorize sharing a private film package.
