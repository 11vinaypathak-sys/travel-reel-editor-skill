# From captures to a memory film

## Find the thread

Inventory, then inspect. Sort promising moments by what they contribute: arrival, place, texture, action, connection or release. These are questions, not mandatory chapters. A technically good shot can still say little in the current story.

Open on a clear visual event or feeling. An actual action may establish a journey more directly than an introductory photograph. End for emotional closure rather than file order.

## Shape rhythm with music

| Direction | Useful tendency | What to protect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tranquil diary | Longer holds, fewer transitions, room for atmosphere | The small event that makes a quiet shot worth holding |
| Punchy reel | Short cuts around musical accents, occasional pauses | Legibility and an understandable journey |
| Photographic film | Composed stills, selective movement, deliberate contrasts | Crop, hierarchy and meaningful sequence |

These are starting tendencies. A beat grid does not require a cut on every beat. Let actions resolve and photographs become readable. A hold can make the next faster section feel stronger.

Record integer frame boundaries. Map musical beats to frames, then adjust to the recording's actual phrase and offset. BPM alone cannot establish synchronization.

## Create one visual language

Correct exposure and white balance before styling. Compare neighbouring shots, especially skin, sky and dark foliage. One LUT intensity rarely gives every source the same temperament. Separate the shared look from individual correction where possible.

Stabilization trades frame area and sometimes natural movement for steadiness. Review subjects and edges after treatment. Rolling shutter, perspective distortion and motion blur can remain. Do not present a tracking metric as proof of recovered detail.

## Make photographs belong

| Layout | When it helps | Common failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full-screen image with restrained border | One strong composition deserves attention | Accidental subject crop or partial baked caption |
| Large paired frames | Two views create contrast or continuation | Two equally small pictures floating in unused space |
| Contact sheet with a dominant view | A place benefits from related details | Too many competing panels or hierarchy lost on a phone |

Keeping every pixel is not always the strongest composition, but important subjects and the user's exclusions take priority. Inspect crops at the final aspect ratio and plausible viewing size.

Choose one motion: a quiet push, shared drift, simple reveal or short transition. Avoid stacking rotation, scale, slide and camera movement by default. A film treatment can be subtle; it does not need constant sprockets, noise or shutter sound.

## Iterate on the specific feedback

Use a compact style proof with an opening, a photo treatment, a demanding motion shot and a sound transition. Capture feedback by time and intent: replace a source, enlarge images, simplify motion, or keep music continuous.

When a user approves colour but rejects layout, retain the colour and change the layout. Preserve a previous version until the new delivery is checked or cleanup is requested.

## Share the process separately

A public process needs no original captures, trip dates, identities, raw chats, local paths or commercial assets. Use synthetic or clearly licensed examples. Inspect the exact staged files and commit history before publication.
